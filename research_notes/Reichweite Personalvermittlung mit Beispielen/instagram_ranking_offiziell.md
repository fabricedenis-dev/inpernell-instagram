# Official Instagram/Meta statements on reach and ranking (Reels, carousels, single posts, Stories), research status 21 Nov 2026

Scope: what Instagram/Meta itself says (Mosseri, creators.instagram.com, about.instagram.com, help center), plus reporting on it. Each claim is dated. **[OLD]** = source from before 2025, possibly outdated. **[UNVERIFIED]** = only secondary or blog sources.

## 1. Main ranking signals (watch time, likes/sends per reach, saves, comments) and how each area works

### Takeaway
Mosseri's official core statement (Jan 2025) still stands: **the three most important signals are watch time, likes and sends, each measured per reach**. Sends (sharing via DM) count slightly more for reaching non-followers, likes count slightly more for followers. I found no official 2025/26 statement that comments or saves are top signals. Each area (Feed, Stories, Reels, Explore) has its own algorithm. Reels and Explore show mostly content from accounts people don't follow.

### Cited Findings
- Mosseri, 22 Jan 2025 (video series "performance in 2025"): "The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends." / "So when looking at your insights, pay close attention to average watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach." / "Likes are slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content." (connected = followers, unconnected = non-followers) — [Social Media Today, 22.01.2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/); same 3 signals reported by [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-reveals-key-factors-for-boosting-reach-8646916)
- The claim that a send counts "3 to 5 times" as much as a like is **not** from Mosseri. Blogs call it a "reported estimate" and Instagram does not publish weights — [Kompozy, 25.09.2026](https://kompozy.io/news/instagram-mosseri-ranking-signals-guidance) **[UNVERIFIED]**
- Reports that Mosseri said in Oct 2026 that Instagram focuses "a lot more" on sends than on comments: only a single blog source, not confirmed — [Zoomsphere / search summary](https://www.zoomsphere.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-what-actually-gets-reach) **[UNVERIFIED]**
- Insights reportedly gained a "share rate" metric in Apr 2026, close to "sends per reach" — [Keywords Everywhere Tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/) **[UNVERIFIED]**
- Official explainer "Instagram Ranking Explained" (Mosseri, **31 May 2023 [OLD]**, still the latest official overview I found): each area (Feed, Stories, Explore, Reels, Search) has its own algorithm. Most important signals in rough order:
  - Feed: "Your activity", "Information about the post", "Information about the person who posted", "Your history of interacting with someone".
  - Stories: "Viewing history", "Engagement history", "Closeness". Stories are mostly about accounts people already follow.
  - Explore: "Information about the post" (popularity/engagement matters much more here than in Feed/Stories), "Your activity in Explore", interaction history, information about the account.
  - Reels: "Your activity", interaction history, "Information about the reel", "Information about the person who posted". Reels are meant to entertain, and most content comes from accounts people don't follow.
  — [about.instagram.com, 31.05.2023](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- Recommendations work in stages: every eligible post is first shown to a small test audience (followers and non-followers), and well-performing posts then go to larger audiences — [creators.instagram.com „Helping Creators Find New Audiences", 30.04.2024 [OLD]](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality)
- Stories vs. Feed: according to reports, Mosseri said in Jan 2025 that for most creators, feed posts (incl. Reels/carousels) are the best way to reach many people, while Stories are better for their own engaged community — [RouteNote summary](https://routenote.com/blog/how-often-you-should-post-on-instagram/) **[UNVERIFIED wording]**
- The Meta Transparency Center pages on Reels ranking (transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/ig-reels) returned 404 on 21 Nov 2026 and could not be checked.

### Inferences
- For a ~935-follower account aiming to reach non-followers, the most important metric is **sends per reach** (content people forward to a friend: concrete job info, salary comparisons, checklists). Second is **average watch time** (Reels) or dwell time (carousels).
- Stories hardly reach new audiences. They are for loyalty and follower contact.
- Comments and saves are not named as top signals in the official 2025 statement. They may help, but there is no official weighting.

### Gaps
- I found no updated official ranking explainer from 2025/2026 (system cards on transparency.meta.com could not be reached).
- No official statement on how saves are weighted.

## 2. Originality: unoriginal/reposted content, watermarks, templates, repetition

### Takeaway
Since **30 Apr 2026** Instagram has applied its originality rules for Reels (from 2024) to **photos and carousels** too. Accounts that mostly repost others' content (aggregators) are excluded from all recommendations. Followers still see them. Watermarks, borders, subtitles or a credit in the caption alone do **not** make content original. Templates are only OK if materially edited. There is no official rule saying "repeated own templates = less reach".

### Cited Findings
- 30 Apr 2026 (official): accounts that mainly post unoriginal content "will no longer be shown in places where we recommend content". Followed accounts are not affected. Check is on a rolling 30-day basis: eligibility returns when "a significant percentage" of recent photos, carousels and reels are original. No fixed numeric threshold is given — [creators.instagram.com „Rewarding original creators", 30.04.2026](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram)
- Counts as original: "Photos or videos you took", "Content you designed", and third-party content with meaningful additions ("adding unique text on the content itself", "adding creative graphics that offer new information", Remix). Self-check: does the edit add value beyond "simply restating or referencing"? — [same source](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram)
- Does **not** count as original: "Re-uploading someone else's work" and edits that only add "a border, watermark, subtitles, or a credit in the captions" — [same source](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram). Low-effort edits such as watermarks or speed changes, and screenshots of other posts (even with credit), also don't count — [TechCrunch, 30.04.2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/)
- Meme templates/popular clips are OK when something is added (humor, commentary, own text, voiceover, creative edit) — [TechCrunch, 30.04.2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/)
- Recommended alternatives for sharing others' content: Collabs, Remix, sharing to Story, Reposts. Publishers with licences/permissions are exempt — [Social Media Today, 30.04.2026](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-expands-originality-reach-penalties/819016/)
- Account Status shows eligibility, with an option to appeal — [creators.instagram.com, 30.04.2026](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram)
- Older rule (Reels, 30 Apr 2024 [OLD]): with identical copies only the original is recommended; for "relatively new" originals, the copy gets a label linking to the original. Recommendable content "has no visible watermarks and satisfies our recommendation guidelines" — [creators.instagram.com, 30.04.2024](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality)
- 2023 explainer [OLD]: Instagram makes these Reels less visible: low resolution, watermarked, muted, with borders, "mostly text", or "already posted on Instagram" — [about.instagram.com, 31.05.2023](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- Secondary sources call the rule "10+ unoriginal posts in 30 days" for Reels from 2024. The official 2026 page gives no number — [PetaPixel, 30.04.2026](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/) **[number UNVERIFIED]**

### Inferences
- Own designs in your own branding (e.g. Navy/Orange folders, your own job data) clearly count as "content you designed", so there is no originality risk.
- Risks: reposting third-party videos/memes, TikTok exports with watermarks, reels that are "mostly text" or muted (2023 rule, possibly still in effect), posting the same reel twice.
- "Template fatigue" (same layout every time) is not officially penalized, but it can lower watch time and sends, which hurts reach indirectly.

### Gaps
- No official statement on penalties for repetitive own templates.
- Whether "mostly text" reels are still downranked in 2026 is not confirmed (source from 2023).

## 3. AI content: "AI info" labels and distribution

### Takeaway
I found **no** official Meta statement that content labeled "AI info" gets less reach. The only confirmed reach penalty (from **31 Aug 2026**) applies to **profiles that show an AI-generated person and don't label themselves "AI-generated profile"**. Using AI for editing, captions or graphics does not require that label.

### Cited Findings
- 31 Aug 2026: Instagram renamed the "AI creator" label to "AI-generated profile". It is for profiles whose presented person is "generated or substantially created with AI". Profiles without this label may see their reach reduced. Labeled profiles are not penalized just for that. Not needed for AI photo editing, caption polishing, graphics or other creative tweaks. Recommendation eligibility returns by adding the label, and appeals go through Account Status — [TechCrunch, 31.08.2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/instagram-puts-new-limits-on-undisclosed-ai-profiles/); [Business Today, 01.09.2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/artificial-intelligence/story/no-ai-label-no-reach-instagram-cracks-down-on-ai-generated-influencers-552520-2026-09-01); [Engadget](https://engadget.com/2246914/instagram-will-demote-ai-generated-influencers-if-they-dont-clearly-label-their-account/)
- Meta earlier moved the "AI info" label for AI-*edited* content into the post menu. It stays visible on fully AI-generated content (date not checked) — [Engadget summary](https://engadget.com/2246914/instagram-will-demote-ai-generated-influencers-if-they-dont-clearly-label-their-account/) **[date UNVERIFIED]**
- Mosseri's year-end post (31 Dec 2025 / 1 Jan 2026, Threads/Instagram): "Authenticity is becoming infinitely reproducible". Raw, imperfect content is becoming a sign of authenticity, and verification of real content (e.g. labeling at the moment of capture) is under consideration. This is an outlook, not a ranking rule — [Om Malik, 01.01.2026](https://om.co/2026/01/01/what-is-instagrams-adam-mosseri-really-saying-in-his-year-end-memo/); [PetaPixel, 02.01.2026](https://petapixel.com/2026/01/02/instagram-head-calls-out-camera-companies-for-going-in-the-wrong-direction); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-outlines-the-challenges-of-ai-content/808674/)

### Inferences
- For Inpernell: AI-generated characters (e.g. comic sketches with invented people) are not an AI profile as long as the account doesn't present a fake person as its "face". Labeling individual AI posts as AI info is harmless according to everything I found.
- Mosseri's outlook favours real, human content (real team, real office) as a trust signal. This is a trend statement, not a proven ranking factor.
- Note: a voice that imitates a real person is a separate issue (Meta rules on manipulated media; also CLAUDE.md: no voice cloning).

### Gaps
- I could not find the current wording of Meta's "AI info" help page (2025/26) on whether labeled content is treated differently in recommendations.

## 4. Formats and practical points: Trial Reels, posting frequency, carousels/music, hashtags, keywords, length, hook, links

### Takeaway
Officially confirmed: **Trial Reels** for everyone (June 2025); **at most 5 hashtags** per post/Reel (Dec 2025); Reels **over 3 minutes are not recommended to non-followers**; there is **no official "right" posting frequency**. Carousels showing up in the Reels feed with music, hook guidance (first 3 seconds) and keyword-SEO claims are only secondary reporting or blog advice.

### Cited Findings
- **Trial Reels**: launched Dec 2024. These reels are shown to non-followers first, don't appear on the grid/Reels tab, and can be shared with everyone afterwards (toggle "Trial" before sharing) — [Meta Newsroom, 10.12.2024 [OLD]](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfo/); [creators.instagram.com](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels). Available to everyone since June 2025 — [about.instagram.com „Inspiring Creativity", June 2025](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/inspiring-instagram-creativity); [BetaNews, 14.07.2025](https://betanews.com/2025/07/14/instagram-opens-up-trial-reels-feature-to-more-creators/). Followers can still see a trial reel if a non-follower sends it to them — [Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/instagram-trial-reels/)
- **Posting frequency**: Mosseri, March 2025: "there's no perfect number". Some successful accounts post once a month, others five reels a day. Posting more should not come at the cost of creativity/well-being — [RouteNote](https://routenote.com/blog/how-often-you-should-post-on-instagram/); [Kontentino](https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/). Commonly quoted "2 feed posts/week + 2 stories/day" comes from 2021 [OLD]. Buffer data (2M+ posts, not Meta) shows a positive link between frequency and follower growth — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/)
- **Carousels in the Reels feed with music**: Mosseri reportedly told Colin and Samir that carousels with music can appear in the Reels viewer, and a carousel someone skipped may be shown again starting with slide 2 — [Uppbeat Blog](https://uppbeat.io/blog/creator-news/whats-working-on-instagram-right-now-carousels-and-sends-says-adam-mosseri) **[UNVERIFIED, no official Instagram source found; the slide-2 behaviour was reported before 2025]**
- **Hashtags**: since 19 Dec 2025 at most 5 hashtags per post and reel (previously 30), announced via @Creators after tests with 3. Reasoning: fewer, more targeted hashtags work better than many generic ones. According to reports, Mosseri says hashtags are no longer a main driver of reach (no direct quote) — [Business Today, 19.12.2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/no-more-hashtag-stuffing-instagram-limits-hashtag-usage-to-just-5-per-reel-and-post-507444-2025-12-19); [BetaNews, 19.12.2025](https://betanews.com/2025/12/19/instagram-puts-a-limit-on-hashtag-usage/); [onlinemarketing.de](https://onlinemarketing.de/social-media-marketing/hashtag-limit-instagram). Whether hashtags in the first comment count toward the limit is **unconfirmed** — [RecurPost](https://recurpost.com/instagram-scheduler/instagram-hashtag-limits-and-placement-rules/)
- **Keywords/captions**: reports say Instagram prioritizes caption keywords, on-screen text and visual content over hashtags — [Business Today, 19.12.2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/no-more-hashtag-stuffing-instagram-limits-hashtag-usage-to-just-5-per-reel-and-post-507444-2025-12-19) **[paraphrase, no official quote]**. Mosseri named captions/translations as a reach tactic — [search summary, Buffer/Later](https://buffer.com/library/instagram-reels-length) **[UNVERIFIED]**
- **Reels length**: reels can be up to 20 minutes, but anything over 3 minutes (180 s) is not recommended to new audiences. Instagram shows a warning above 180 s — [Buffer, 2.7M reels analysis](https://buffer.com/library/instagram-reels-length); [Socialinsider 2026](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-length/). Optimal length is disputed (Buffer: 90 s to 3 min; Socialinsider H1 2026: 45 to 60 s for new audiences) and these are **not Meta statements**.
- **Hook / first 3 seconds**: no official Meta source found. Only blogs claim the first seconds decide whether a reel gets wider distribution — [Kompozy, 25.09.2026](https://kompozy.io/news/instagram-mosseri-ranking-signals-guidance) **[UNVERIFIED]**. Officially, average watch time counts as a top signal (see section 1), which supports a strong opening indirectly.
- **Engagement prompts**: Instagram clarified (2024 [OLD]) that calls to action only count as engagement bait if the goal is just to "hack" reach. Recommendation guidelines had not changed — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-advice-single-word-ctas-longer-reels/718151/)

### Inferences
- For Inpernell: (a) test Trial Reels deliberately for new topics; (b) up to 5 specific hashtags (regional + occupation), which matches the CLAUDE.md rule; (c) first caption line and on-screen text with search terms (e.g. "Job Lagerlogistik [Stadt]"); (d) keep reels under 3 minutes, in practice well under 90 s; (e) put music on carousels too, since it is cheap and at worst neutral.
- CTA format: ask an open question (matches CLAUDE.md) rather than "comment X", to avoid engagement bait.

### Gaps
- **Link in bio vs. links**: I found no official 2025/26 Meta statement on reach effects of links in captions/stories or of "link in bio" CTAs. Not researched further (tool limit).
- No official Meta guidance on the "first 3 seconds".
- Carousel/music in the Reels feed has no primary source.

## 5. Recommendation eligibility: what makes content ineligible

### Takeaway
Instagram's **Recommendation Guidelines** are stricter than the Community Guidelines. Content that breaks them is still shown to followers but **not to non-followers** (Explore, Reels, feed suggestions). Repeated breaches make the **whole account** ineligible for a period of time. Status can be checked in **Account Status**.

### Cited Findings
- Posts that break the Recommendation Guidelines stay allowed, but Instagram avoids recommending them. With repeated breaches "none of its posts are eligible for recommendation for a period of time". Account Status shows eligibility, with an appeal option — [about.instagram.com, 31.05.2023 [OLD]](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- Categories that may not be recommended: self-harm/eating disorders, violence, sexually suggestive content, "Content that is associated with low-quality publishing", misleading health/finance claims, content debunked by fact-checkers — [creators.instagram.com, 30.04.2024 [OLD]](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality)
- Content users broadly dislike, such as **clickbait and engagement bait**, is not recommended — [Instagram Help Center: Recommendation Guidelines](https://help.instagram.com/313829416281232) (page content could not be loaded on 21 Nov 2026, so this comes from the search snippet); see also [Guidelines for Content Lowered in Feed and Stories](https://help.instagram.com/1615012435678060)
- Meta (all apps): content with "limited originality" that is mainly repurposed may get less distribution — [Meta Transparency Center: Lowering distribution of problematic content](https://transparency.meta.com/enforcement/taking-action/lowering-distribution-of-problematic-content/) (snippet, date not checked)
- Creator advice: don't post reels with watermarks, blurry or low-quality reels — [creators.instagram.com, Recommendations eligibility tips](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators) (snippet, date not checked)
- Reels over 3 minutes are not recommended to non-followers (see section 4). Aggregators/unoriginal content are excluded (section 2, from 30 Apr 2026 also for photos/carousels). Unlabeled AI-generated profiles get reduced reach (section 3, from 31 Aug 2026).

### Inferences
- Checklist for each Inpernell post: own content, no third-party watermark, sharp/high resolution, not muted (always with sound, matches CLAUDE.md), no "comment JOB" bait, no misleading salary or finance claims (only primary sources), under 3 minutes, at most 5 hashtags. Check Account Status regularly.
- "Misleading finance content" could become relevant for salary/wage claims, so the CLAUDE.md rule of only using primary sources with dates also protects reach.

### Gaps
- The current full text of the Instagram Recommendation Guidelines (help.instagram.com/313829416281232) could not be loaded (empty page). I could not check whether categories changed in 2025/26.
- Meta Transparency Center system cards (Feed/Reels/Explore/Stories) returned 404 and were not checked.
