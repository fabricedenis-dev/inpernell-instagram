# Recruiting-Content auf Instagram (organisch, Deutschland, Personalberatung/-vermittlung für Fachkräfte)

Research date: 2026-11-18. Quality labels used: **[Studie/Daten]** = primary or survey data; **[Plattform]** = statement from Meta/Instagram or Mosseri (often reported second-hand); **[Ratgeber/Meinung]** = trade media, vendor blog or opinion with no data. Older data is labelled with its year.

## 1. Job ads vs. employer-branding content (people, behind-the-scenes, stories, day-in-the-life Reels), and ratio recommendations

### Takeaway
No German study measures Instagram job posts against employer-branding posts directly. The trade-media consensus is "don't flood the feed with job ads": keep openings in a pinned "Jobs" highlight and fill the feed with people, storytelling and Q&As. The Instagram algorithm rewards watch time and sends, which plain job graphics rarely earn. I found no source for a fixed 80/20 ratio; it is a rule of thumb only.

### Cited Findings
- [Ratgeber] ingenieur.de (Apr 2025, based on a VDI Service-Talk with HAMBURG WASSER) recommends day-in-the-life storytelling, behind-the-scenes, team success stories, Q&A stickers (e.g. a civil engineer answering questions at a set time), announced Lives, employee profiles and the CEO on camera. It says open positions belong in a pinned "Jobs" Story Highlight linked to the career page, to avoid "Job-Spam". It lists these mistakes: "excessive advertising and job posts", inauthentic content, little interaction, an unclear target group, only job content, and irregular activity. It gives **no ratio recommendation** — [ingenieur.de](https://www.ingenieur.de/karriere/arbeitsleben/fachkraefte-finden-auf-instagram-so-kann-es-gehen/)
- [Ratgeber] Recommends answering comments and DMs within hours, ideally right away, and posting regularly without long gaps — [ingenieur.de](https://www.ingenieur.de/karriere/arbeitsleben/fachkraefte-finden-auf-instagram-so-kann-es-gehen/)
- [Studie/Daten, Softgarden survey, date not confirmed in source] 81% of applicants call employer credibility "very important" in the application phase. Employers' social media presences rank **last** for credibility, with 43% finding them "(rather) not credible". Image films and social media presences are often seen as staged — [Computerwoche](https://www.computerwoche.de/article/2835070/arbeitgeber-patzen-im-recruiting-prozess.html)
- [Ratgeber] Mittelstand guide: applications come mainly when candidates learn early and clearly what to expect, "not because the video is pretty". It puts a professional recruiting video at roughly €5–10k — [collectivebrain.de](https://collectivebrain.de/recruiting-videos-mittelstand-imagefilm-bewerber-gewinnen/)
- [Ratgeber] Short-form video (day-in-the-life, employee interviews) is called the most effective B2B Instagram format — [HubSpot DE](https://blog.hubspot.de/marketing/instagram-b2b-marketing). Reels ideas listed: behind-the-scenes (e.g. a warehouse in the morning), talking head, screen recording, low budget (€0–500/month) — [skill-sprinters.de](https://skill-sprinters.de/blog/social-media/instagram-reels-fuer-unternehmen-2026/)
- [Ratgeber] Reels are not equally suitable for every industry and must match the target group's interests and values. Music warning: since 19 Oct 2022 only private persons may use Instagram's music library in Reels, so business accounts face a risk of Abmahnung (status 2023, re-check) — [praktischarzt.de](https://www.praktischarzt.de/magazin/arbeitgeber/instagram-reels-ein-geeignetes-recruiting-tool/)
- [Ratgeber] Trends named: Instagram Stories and employees as testimonials — [trusted.de](https://trusted.de/social-media-recruiting). Hays: people use Instagram mainly privately, which is exactly why recruiters should reach passive candidates there — [Hays](https://www.hays.de/personaldienstleistung/recruiting-tipps/social-media-recruiting)
- [Plattform] Mosseri (Jan 2025, reported second-hand) named watch time, likes per reach and **sends per reach** as the top three Reels ranking signals. Likes matter slightly more for followers and sends slightly more for non-followers — [CreatorFlow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/instagram-algorithm-shift-why-sends-matter-more-than-ever/521389/). Mosseri also said carousels and sends are "what's working" — [Uppbeat](https://uppbeat.io/blog/creator-news/whats-working-on-instagram-right-now-carousels-and-sends-says-adam-mosseri)
- [Plattform] Original creators get more distribution than reposters, and accounts that mostly repost are not recommended to non-followers — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources//instagram-algorithms/)

### Inferences
- A plain "Wir suchen CNC-Fräser (m/w/d)" graphic offers little watch time and little reason to send it on, so by Instagram's stated signals it is unlikely to reach non-followers. A specific job does create a send reason ("this is something for my brother-in-law"). Job posts should therefore be written to be forwarded, e.g. "Kennst du jemanden, der …?" with concrete facts such as salary, location and shift model.
- Combining the low credibility of employer social media (Softgarden) with the "people, not ads" advice suggests that authentic recruiter and candidate faces beat polished image content. This matters especially for an agency, which can't show its "own" shop floor.
- An 80/20 or 70/30 split of value/people content to job posts is a sensible working hypothesis, but it is unsourced.

### Gaps
- No German study measuring applications per post type (job graphic vs. employee Reel vs. carousel).
- No source for the "80/20" ratio. The Uni Bamberg "Recruiting Trends" social media figures found are from the 2020 edition; I found no newer Bamberg Instagram data.
- I found no 2024–2026 Softgarden study focused on Instagram. Softgarden's 2025 surveys (~7,000 applicants) focus on AI use — [ingenieur.de](https://www.ingenieur.de/karriere/bewerbung/ki-und-karriere-wie-kuenstliche-intelligenz-den-bewerbungsprozess-praegt/)

## 2. Salary ranges in job ads (Germany) and the EU Pay Transparency Directive

### Takeaway
Strong survey evidence shows salary information raises willingness to apply in Germany: 61–68% want or prefer it, and about 45% might drop out when pay is vague. Yet only about 12% of German job ads show pay, the lowest share in a European comparison. Showing a range is a clear differentiator. The EU Directive's transposition deadline (7 June 2026) has passed and Germany has not yet passed a law (status Nov 2026). The directive requires disclosure **before the interview / before the contract**, not necessarily in the ad.

### Cited Findings
- [Studie/Daten, Indeed Hiring Lab, May 2026] Only **12%** of German job ads include pay (about 1 in 8), versus UK 56%, NL 48%, FR 43%, IE 39%, IT 36%, ES 17%. Hourly-wage ads state an exact amount more often (39% vs. 14% for monthly salaries). Median range width is about 23% of the lower bound for monthly salaries (e.g. €2,000–2,460) — [Indeed Hiring Lab](https://hiringlab.indeed.com/de/blog/2026/05/14/vollstaendige-gehaltstransparenz-ist-in-europa-noch-zukunftsmusik/)
- [Studie/Daten, Indeed/Censuswide survey, Jan 2025, n=6,011 across six countries] In Germany **61%** would be more likely to respond to a job with a pay range (up to 82% in Ireland). 64–83% say ads should include pay by default, and women agree more often than men — [Indeed Hiring Lab](https://hiringlab.indeed.com/de/blog/2026/05/14/vollstaendige-gehaltstransparenz-ist-in-europa-noch-zukunftsmusik/)
- [Studie/Daten, Indeed Hiring Lab, Mar 2025] Pay disclosure in Germany is highest in cleaning services (~41%, end of 2024) and below 10% in well-paid fields such as software development and law — [Hiring Lab 2025](https://www.hiringlab.org/de/blog/2025/03/05/gehaltsangaben-bleiben-in-deutschland-die-ausnahme/); [Nordkurier](https://www.nordkurier.de/wirtschaft/grosses-geheimnis-um-gehalt-in-stellenanzeigen-nur-eine-branche-ist-vorbildlich-3383590). Pay transparency is especially low in IT job ads — [channelpartner.de](https://www.channelpartner.de/article/4179829/gehaltstransparenz-bei-it-jobangeboten-besonders-gering.html)
- [Studie/Daten, Königsteiner Gruppe/Bilendi, Sept 2025, n=1,028 employees] With vague salary wording, 16% would definitely not apply and 29% would reconsider, so about **45%** are at risk. 68% want concrete figures in the ad and 82% expect at least an approximate range at first contact. Phrases like "attraktives Gehalt" deter applicants — [Personalwirtschaft, 6 Mar 2026](https://www.personalwirtschaft.de/?p=201402)
- [Studie/Daten, StepStone Gehaltsstudie 2026, cited second-hand] **86%** say transparent salary info in ads improves their opinion of the company — [Personalwirtschaft](https://www.personalwirtschaft.de/?p=201402)
- [Studie/Daten, StepStone, 2024/2025, second-hand] Every second respondent has already decided not to apply because the ad named no salary. About 60% of candidates want salary info in ads (StepStone Arbeitgeberattraktivität report) — [tageskarte.io](https://www.tageskarte.io/zahlen/detail/5-gute-gruende-die-fuer-gehaltsangaben-in-stellenanzeigen-sprechen.html); [StepStone page](https://stepstone.de/e-recruiting/hr-wissen/gehalt/stellenangebote-mit-gehaltsangabe) (StepStone page returned 503 at research time). t3n reports that salary info demonstrably raises applicant numbers — [t3n](https://t3n.de/news/?p=1601397)
- [Recht] Directive (EU) 2023/970, Art. 5: applicants must be told the starting pay or pay range before the contract (the Hiring Lab wording is "before the interview"), and asking about salary history is restricted. Some guides claim a general obligation to put pay in every ad; others say the ad need not contain it. Treat this as unresolved until a German law exists — [boerse-express](https://www.boerse-express.com/news/articles/eu-entgelttransparenzrichtlinie-deutschland-ohne-umsetzungsgesetz-921477); [Händlerbund "Pflicht oder Mythos"](https://ohn.haendlerbund.de/recht/arbeitsrecht/gehaltsangaben-stellenanzeigen-pflicht-mythos); [IHK Karlsruhe](https://www.ihk.de/karlsruhe/fachthemen/recht/arbeitsrecht/aktuelle-rechtsprechung/entgelttransparenzrichtlinie-6784512)
- [Recht/Status] Germany missed the 7 June 2026 deadline. An expert commission reported in Oct 2025; a cabinet decision was expected "possibly in August" 2026 — [Personalwirtschaft](https://www.personalwirtschaft.de/?p=203783). A Nov 2026 piece says there is still no Referentenentwurf — [Verfassungsblog](https://verfassungsblog.de/?p=108014)
- Conflict: Personalwirtschaft (Mar 2026) says pre-contract disclosure applies to companies with **100+ employees**. The Directive's Art. 5 pre-employment disclosure applies to employers regardless of size; the size thresholds concern gender pay gap reporting. One guide also states explicitly that there is no size threshold — [Personalwirtschaft](https://www.personalwirtschaft.de/?p=201402) vs. [boerse-express](https://www.boerse-express.com/news/articles/entgelttransparenz-gehaltsspannen-in-stellenausschreibungen-jetzt-pflicht-921920). The 100+ claim looks wrong.

### Inferences
- For an agency, a salary range in the post ("€55–65k + Schichtzulage") is one of the strongest levers. It is rare in Germany (12%) and the skilled-worker target group explicitly asks for it. Showing a range in the hook or on the first carousel slide differentiates.
- An agency must agree the range with its client. The directive obligations fall on the employer, but agencies that pass on the range early position themselves well for the coming law.

### Gaps
- No German data measuring actual application uplift on Instagram posts with vs. without salary (all data comes from surveys or job-board ads).
- Final German law text and its start date were unknown as of Nov 2026.

## 3. Faces vs. graphics: do real people increase engagement?

### Takeaway
The best-known evidence is old (2014) but large. Instagram photos with faces got about 38% more likes and about 32% more comments. Trade sources consistently recommend real employees and recruiters on camera. I found no 2024–2026 replication specific to recruiting.

### Cited Findings
- [Studie/Daten, Georgia Tech + Yahoo Labs, CHI 2014, ~1.1M Instagram photos] Photos with faces got **38% more likes** and **32% more comments**. The number of faces, age and gender made no difference. Heavy posting reduced feedback per photo — [Georgia Tech News](https://www.news.gatech.edu/news/2014/03/20/face-it-instagram-pictures-faces-are-more-popular); [ScienceDaily](https://sciencedaily.com/releases/2014/03/140320134724.htm). **Label: 2014 data, pre-Reels algorithm.**
- [Ratgeber] Real employee voices, appealing images and short everyday-work videos are recommended — [drweb.de](https://www.drweb.de/best-practices-social-media-recruiting/); employees as testimonials — [trusted.de](https://trusted.de/social-media-recruiting)
- [Studie/Daten, Softgarden] Low credibility of employer social media (43% not credible) — [Computerwoche](https://www.computerwoche.de/article/2835070/arbeitgeber-patzen-im-recruiting-prozess.html)

### Inferences
- For a recruiting agency, the recruiter's face (talking-head Reel: "Ich suche gerade einen CNC-Dreher in Villingen …") plus real candidate or client-employee faces (with consent) is the most evidence-aligned design. Text-only job tiles should at least carry a person.

### Gaps
- No recent (2024–2026) peer-reviewed or large-scale data on faces vs. graphics for Reels or carousels, or for recruiting content specifically.

## 4. Local relevance: regional hashtags, place names, dialect/identity

### Takeaway
There is practitioner advice to use location tags and regional hashtags (e.g. #JobinDüsseldorf), but I found no quantitative evidence. Hashtags have lost importance: Instagram ended hashtag following (announced for late 2024).

### Cited Findings
- [Ratgeber] Recommends location tags to reach local job-seekers and regional hashtags such as #JobinDüsseldorf, plus #jobs and #stellefrei. Opinions on hashtags differ. Instagram announced the end of following hashtags from late 2024 — [ingenieur.de](https://www.ingenieur.de/karriere/arbeitsleben/fachkraefte-finden-auf-instagram-so-kann-es-gehen/)
- [Ratgeber, anecdotal] A social recruiting campaign brought an average of two regional applications per day (no company, period or channel details) — [zeeg.me](https://zeeg.me/de/blog/content/social-recruiting-erfolgreiche-talentsuche-uber-soziale-medien)

### Inferences
- Skilled workers rarely relocate, so a place name in the first line or on the cover ("Job in Rottweil", "Schwarzwald") filters instantly and makes the post forwardable to local friends (sends). This is a plausible mechanism, but untested.
- Dialect or regional identity (e.g. Badisch/Schwäbisch phrases) can build authenticity. I found no evidence.

### Gaps
- No data on regional hashtags or place names in hooks vs. engagement or applications; no data on dialect use.

## 5. Hooks, captions, Du vs. Sie, CTA, comment-to-DM, DM as application channel, mobile/no-cover-letter application

### Takeaway
"Du" vs. "Sie" has little measured effect on applications. Fit with the company culture matters more, and in Germany ads are split about half and half. Comment-to-DM automations via official Meta partner tools appear permissible if the DM delivers what was promised and isn't spammy. Explicit "comment X" engagement-bait rules from Meta could not be confirmed from a primary source. Low-friction application is strongly supported: 55% of skilled workers see the cover letter as a hurdle, and about half have abandoned a mobile application (older data).

### Cited Findings
- [Studie/Daten] Job ads are split roughly half Du, half Sie, and the question seems unimportant to many applicants (Trendence vs. Forsa use different methods) — [Personalwirtschaft](https://www.personalwirtschaft.de/?p=163251). No form of address is universally right; 28% favour Du in ads — [Personalwirtschaft](https://www.personalwirtschaft.de/?p=171179). Indeed: Du in job ads has risen sharply (doubled in three years) — [Personalwirtschaft](https://www.personalwirtschaft.de/?p=95938). A survey of 1,011 employees rejects ordered Duzen — [Personalwirtschaft](https://www.personalwirtschaft.de/?p=99372). [2017] For most applicants Du/Sie is not a decision criterion — [CareerBuilder PDF](https://careerbuilder-germany-gmbh.mynewsdesk.com/news/du-oder-sie-die-kunst-der-richtigen-kandidaten-ansprache-im-recruiting-242213.pdf). [Meinung] Performance-oriented candidates may prefer Sie — [Haufe](https://www.haufe.de/personal/hr-management/siezen-oder-duzen_80_484682.html)
- [Studie/Daten, meinestadt.de] **55%** of skilled workers with vocational training call the cover letter a hurdle (sample reported as 1,034 or 1,520, inconsistent) — [Deutsche Handwerks Zeitung](https://www.deutsche-handwerks-zeitung.de/so-senken-sie-die-huerden-zur-bewerbung/150/3099/366301); [Haufe](https://www.haufe.de/personal/hr-management/bewerbung-warum-anschreiben-das-recruiting-erschweren_80_431376.html)
- [Studie/Daten, meinestadt.de Mobile Recruiting Studie, **2017**] Almost half have abandoned an application because it was too cumbersome or impossible on a smartphone, and 76% of skilled workers prefer the smartphone for job search — [t3n](https://t3n.de/news/klappt-mobile-recruiting-1096387/)
- [Studie/Daten, Indeed, **2016**] 42% had abandoned an application because the process was too complicated — [Indeed Bewerbungsstudie PDF](https://www.presseportal.de/en/download/document/597898ff450000c60e74207d-indeed-bewerbungsstudie-final.pdf); [Computerwoche](https://www.computerwoche.de/article/2769024/bewerbung-muss-schnell-und-einfach-gehen.html)
- [Plattform/vendor, second-hand] Comment-to-DM runs through Meta's official messaging API (app review, Business Partners such as ManyChat). Replying to someone who has already commented is within policy; Meta's messaging policy prohibits initiating messages to users who haven't engaged. Grey areas: "keyword bait" where the promised asset isn't delivered, identical link blasts and repetitive content. A reported 750 private replies per hour limit couldn't be verified — [Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/blog/is-comment-to-dm-allowed-on-instagram); [ManyChat rules](https://manychat.com/blog/instagram-dm-automation-rules/); [CreatorFlow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/automated-instagram-dms-creator-rules-safety/); [Ayrshare](https://www.ayrshare.com/?p=16418); [elev8or](https://www.elev8or.io/blog/engage/is-manychat-safe)
- [Ratgeber] Up to five links can now be placed in the Instagram bio — [e-recht24](https://www.e-recht24.de/impressum/13078-impressum-auf-instagram.html)

### Inferences
- "Kommentiere JOB und ich schick dir die Details per DM" via an official tool is acceptable if the DM really contains the job details or a short application link. The post should also carry real value (salary, location), so the comment is not the only purpose. Asking for comments purely to game reach risks being classified as engagement bait (Meta's Facebook engagement-bait guidelines; not verified for Instagram in this research).
- An Instagram DM ("Schick mir einfach 'Interesse' + deinen Beruf") is the lowest-friction first contact. For CNC or industrial workers who dislike cover letters (55%), a DM, WhatsApp or one-page mobile form without a cover letter should convert much better than a career-site ATS link. The supporting data is older, though.
- Du on Instagram fits the platform norm for skilled trades and young workers. For banking advisers or ERP consultants a mixed or neutral style may fit better, and the evidence says the choice matters little.

### Gaps
- No German data on caption length, emoji use or first-line hooks for recruiting posts.
- No primary Meta text found stating whether "comment KEYWORD" CTAs are demoted on Instagram.
- No recent (2024–2026) German data on conversion with vs. without a cover letter or with one-click mobile application.

## 6. Examples of successful German recruiting Instagram accounts and formats

### Takeaway
Searches turned up very few documented German cases with numbers. HAMBURG WASSER is cited as a positive example with its formats (employee stories, Q&A, Jobs highlight). No agency (Personalvermittlung) case study with KPIs was found.

### Cited Findings
- [Ratgeber/Praxis] HAMBURG WASSER (municipal utility) is presented in a VDI Service-Talk as a strong example: employee profiles, CEO appearances, Q&A with engineers, a Jobs highlight — [ingenieur.de](https://www.ingenieur.de/karriere/arbeitsleben/fachkraefte-finden-auf-instagram-so-kann-es-gehen/)
- [Ratgeber] A local newspaper (BNN) sells "Recruiting-Reels" of up to 60 seconds as a product, showing regional demand for the format — [BNN Infoblatt PDF](https://service.bnn.de/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Infoblatt_Instagram_Recruiting_Reel_OP.pdf)
- [Ratgeber] Formats with good results per guides: Reels, Instagram Live for Q&As and virtual office tours — [eddy.com](https://eddy.com/hr-encyclopedia/instagram-recruiting/); [4 Corner Resources](https://4cornerresources.com/blog/recruiting-on-instagram/)
- [Studie/Daten] Instagram reaches mainly Gen Z, while Facebook is stronger among older groups — [Personalwirtschaft Recruiting Guide 2025](https://personalwirtschaft.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Recruiting-Guide-2025-003.pdf) (as summarised in search results)

### Inferences
- Formats that fit a Fachkräfte agency: "Job der Woche" carousels (slide 1: role, place, salary range plus a face; slide 2: tasks/machines such as Heidenhain or Siemens; slide 3: benefits/shift; slide 4: "So bewirbst du dich: DM genügt"). Also recruiter talking-head Reels, "Placement stories" (with consent), and client shop-floor visits as collab posts.

### Gaps
- No verified German agency or Mittelstand Instagram case with reach or application KPIs. Well-known German social recruiting accounts weren't verified in this research.

## 7. Realistic reach of a small B2B recruiting account and how to get first followers

### Takeaway
Benchmarks suggest median Instagram engagement is low (~0.36% across industries, Rival IQ 2025). No staffing-specific benchmark was found. Reach for small accounts comes mainly from non-follower distribution of Reels, plus collab posts that appear on both profiles' audiences. Trial Reels require 1,000+ followers.

### Cited Findings
- [Studie/Daten, Rival IQ 2025] Median Instagram engagement rate across all industries is **0.36%**; Socialinsider 2025 gives about 0.48%. No staffing/recruiting segment was found — [Rival IQ](https://www.rivaliq.com/blog/social-media-industry-benchmark-report/); [inmagic.ai](https://inmagic.ai/instagram-engagement-rate-benchmarks/)
- [Plattform, second-hand] Collab posts appear on both profiles and reach both follower bases. Mosseri reportedly said reach should not drop when the partner is smaller, and that the larger account should be the initiator or original poster — [Kontentino](https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/does-an-instagram-collab-post-hurt-your-reach-if-the-other-account-is-smaller/); [onlinemarketing.de](https://onlinemarketing.de/social-media-marketing/instagram-collabs-nach-posting-algorithmus-insights)
- [Plattform] Trial Reels let you show a Reel to non-followers first (Meta, June 2025). They were later restricted to public accounts with 1,000+ followers, with an extension to smaller accounts being considered. Mosseri says trials almost always get less reach — [Meta/Instagram](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/inspiring-instagram-creativity); [thekeyword.co](https://www.thekeyword.co/news/instagram-expands-trial-reels-to-all-public-creators-with-1k-or-more-followers)
- [Plattform] Content that performs well is shown to progressively more unconnected accounts, which is how very small accounts can go viral — [Kontentino](https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/share-trial-reel-to-followers/)
- [Ratgeber] Hashtag-driven views in post insights are only visible for accounts with 1,000+ followers — [ingenieur.de](https://www.ingenieur.de/karriere/arbeitsleben/fachkraefte-finden-auf-instagram-so-kann-es-gehen/)

### Inferences
- A new agency account will realistically see low hundreds of views per feed post and occasional spikes on Reels. Engagement of about 0.3–1% of followers is normal; this is an estimate, not sourced for recruiting.
- The most promising growth levers: collab posts with client companies (client as co-author, ideally the larger account initiating), recruiters' and employees' personal accounts resharing, and local partners such as sports clubs, Berufsschulen or IHK events. Cross-posting to Facebook reaches the older skilled-worker group (Instagram skews Gen Z).

### Gaps
- No benchmark for German B2B or recruiting accounts under 1,000 followers. No data on Instagram-to-Facebook cross-posting effects for recruiting.

## 8. Legal/compliance hints in Germany (AGG, image rights, Impressum)

### Takeaway
A business Instagram profile needs an Impressum (§ 5 DDG), reachable within two clicks, e.g. via the bio link, or it risks an Abmahnung. Employee photos need voluntary, specific (ideally written) consent that can be revoked. Music from Instagram's library is risky for business accounts. Job text must be non-discriminatory (m/w/d; avoid "junges Team").

### Cited Findings
- [Recht/Ratgeber] Business-use Instagram accounts fall under the Impressum duty, and the threshold is low (linking to a company or service is enough). The Impressum must be reachable within two clicks, and link tools in the bio are acceptable. A missing Impressum can be abgemahnt — [e-recht24](https://www.e-recht24.de/impressum/13078-impressum-auf-instagram.html); [Händlerbund](https://www.haendlerbund.de/de/leistungen/rechtssicherheit/agb-service/impressum/impressum-social-media/impressum-instagram)
- [Recht/Ratgeber] Publishing employee photos needs a legal basis under Art. 6 DSGVO, in practice consent. The BAG requires written consent from employees (other sources: DSGVO itself requires no written form; text form recommended). Consent must be voluntary and the use described specifically (website, social media). It can be revoked at any time (Art. 7(3) DSGVO), after which images must be removed or faces pixelated. Whether leaving the company counts as revocation is disputed — [dr-datenschutz.de](https://www.dr-datenschutz.de/veroeffentlichung-von-mitarbeiterfotos-nur-mit-einwilligung/); [Deutsche Handwerks Zeitung](https://www.deutsche-handwerks-zeitung.de/mitarbeiterfotos-5-regeln-die-arbeitgeber-beachten-muessen/150/32552/399670); [it-recht-kanzlei](https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/belegschaftsfoto-loeschen-arbeitnehmer-ausscheiden.html). One case cites €10,000 damages over an employee photo — [WEKA](https://www.weka.de/betriebsrat-personalrat/mitarbeiterfoto-verursacht-10-000-euro-schadensersatz/)
- [Ratgeber] Avoid wording such as "junges Team" that could exclude older applicants (gender-neutral job-ad guide, 2025) — [TUHH Leitfaden](https://www.tuhh.de/t3resources/tuhh/download/universitaet/Gleichstellung/2025_Leitfaden__fuer__gender__neutrale__Stellenausschreibungen.pdf)
- [Ratgeber] Using the Instagram music library on business accounts risks an Abmahnung (status 2023) — [praktischarzt.de](https://www.praktischarzt.de/magazin/arbeitgeber/instagram-reels-ein-geeignetes-recruiting-tool/)

### Inferences
