# Instagram Reels for Career / Job-Search / Recruiting Content (DACH, 2025–2026): Formats, Topics, Hooks

Stand: Recherche am 18.11.2026. Labels used: **[OFFIZIELL]** = Instagram/Meta or Mosseri quoted; **[STUDIE]** = data study by a tool vendor; **[MEINUNG]** = blog, guide or commentary without primary data. Older data is marked with its year.

## 1. Which Reel content types perform for career/HR accounts, and which German creators/accounts are examples?

### Takeaway
No public dataset breaks down Reel performance by career-content type (job of the day, CV tips, salary and so on), for DACH or anywhere else. That is a clear gap. Two kinds of evidence point the same way. The most successful German career creators work mainly in short-video tips on applications and interviews (karriereguru, Mr. Bewerbung). Instagram's ranking signals are watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach, and both favour practical tips worth saving or sending (Fehler-Listen, Gehalt, Interview questions) over pure job ads. Job-of-the-day reels mainly reach people who already follow the account, with limited share potential. German recruiting guides recommend people and faces (day in the life, Steckbriefe) and warn against "Job-Spam".

### Cited Findings
- **German HR/career creators, ranking by OnlineMarketing.de (7/21 May 2026) [MEINUNG/Media]:** karriereguru (Tobias Jost) is named the most successful HR influencer, with 800,900 TikTok followers, application advice and tips for specific job situations, in short videos. Mr. Bewerbung (Good News Company) has 114,500 on TikTok, covers applications for Gen Z, training and study places and interview tips, in short videos. Silke Koppitz (career advice) has 57,500 YouTube subscribers and covers career reorientation and self-presentation. Alexander Feichtinger (Instagram, 1,333 followers) posts recruiting and leadership-culture "Tipps und Memes". Madeline Mann (Self Made Millennial, YouTube, 446k) covers interview analysis and salary negotiation. The article names "Bewerbungstipps über Gehaltsverhandlungen bis zu New Work … Recruiting Hacks" as the topic spectrum. — [OnlineMarketing.de Top 10 HR-Influencer](https://onlinemarketing.de/karriere/human-resources/top-10-hr-influencer-social-media-recruiting-karriere)
- karriereguru (Tobias Jost) is described as "mainly active on Instagram" with Reels, posts and Stories. This comes from an influencer-marketing data sheet and is weak. — [socialveins karriereguru](https://socialveins.com/influencer/instagram/karriereguru)
- **StepStone:** Its Instagram presence shows "Storys aus dem Arbeitsalltag". Mandy Rilke works as a content creator at The Stepstone Group and has written about applications and careers since 2022. I found no published Reels strategy or engagement data from StepStone. — [StepStone Magazin](https://www.stepstone.de/magazin/); [StepStone Autorin Mandy Rilke](https://www.stepstone.de/e-recruiting/hr-wissen/autor-in/mandy-rilke)
- **karriere.at:** Its social-media-recruiting guide stresses profile, cover image and company description, and uses its own Instagram bio as an example. It gives no Reel format data. — [karriere.at Social Media Recruiting](https://www.karriere.at/c/a/social-media-recruiting)
- **ingenieur.de (08.04.2025), "Fachkräfte finden auf Instagram" [MEINUNG/Media]:**
  - Recommended formats: day in an employee's life, behind the scenes, success stories, Q&A Stories with the question sticker (e.g. a civil engineer answering questions at a fixed time), Lives, a job Highlight with link, employee Steckbriefe, CEO or leadership presence.
  - Guidelines: subtle branding (small logo or slim bar), "kein Job-Spam", no overloaded corporate design, regular activity, replies within hours, regional hashtags such as #JobinDüsseldorf, location tags for local talent.
  - Reels are mentioned only as a way to stay present "ohne ständige Jobposts".
  - Quote (HAMBURG WASSER): "Menschen geben der Marke ein Gesicht, schaffen Vertrauen und machen sie nahbarer."
  - Source: [ingenieur.de](https://www.ingenieur.de/karriere/arbeitsleben/fachkraefte-finden-auf-instagram-so-kann-es-gehen/)
- praktischArzt (recruiting with Reels, last updated in 2023, so older) suggests content ideas such as information about the application process and team-building measures, and stresses authenticity. — [praktischArzt](https://www.praktischarzt.de/magazin/arbeitgeber/instagram-reels-ein-geeignetes-recruiting-tool/)
- **[OFFIZIELL] Mosseri, Jan 2025:** "The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends." He also said: "Likes are slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content." — [Social Media Today, 22.01.2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)
- **[STUDIE] Metricool Instagram-Studie 2026 (24.4M posts, 375k accounts, global, Jan–Feb 2026 vs 2025):**
  - Reels engagement rate was 4.30% (+25%). Shares rose 67%, saves 5%, likes 17% and comments 11%. Average watch time on Reels rose from 4 to 8.5 seconds.
  - Carousel engagement rate was 3.08%, and carousels got 9x more saves than single images. Single images reached 1.99%, with reach down 22%.
  - Posts that ask a question got 36.7% more comments.
  - Posts with hashtags got 31.7% fewer views. The study notes this may be down to post design rather than the hashtags.
  - The study contains no Germany-specific data.
  - Source: [OnlineMarketing.de on Metricool 2026](https://onlinemarketing.de/social-media-marketing/instagram-studie-2026-formate-beste-performance)

### Inferences
- For "one automated Reel per day", **useful tips** are likely to reach non-followers better than pure job ads, because sends matter more for non-follower reach. Examples are "3 Fehler im Lebenslauf", "Diese Frage kommt im Vorstellungsgespräch – so antwortest du", "Gehalt: SAP-Berater in BW vs. Bayern" and "Wusstest du? Kündigungsfrist …". A job reel interests only people who fit the role, though it can be sent to a friend who does ("Kennst du jemanden …").
- A plausible mix for the target groups (industry, IT/ERP, banking, Schwarzwald-Baar/Tuttlingen/Freiburg) is shown below. This is a derived suggestion, not data.
  1. Bewerbungstipp or "3 Fehler" list
  2. Top-Job reel: the day's carousel job told as a short video with hook, salary range and location
  3. Gehalt/Wusstest-du facts
  4. Regional job-market facts, e.g. the Tuttlingen medtech cluster or industry in Schwarzwald-Baar
  5. Myth-busting ("Mythos: Personalvermittler kosten Bewerber Geld")
- Germany's best-performing career creators use **face and voice** (talking head). A fully template-based reel with no face will probably have a harder time. See question 2 (Emplifi: a face or speech in the first 3 seconds improves retention). A practical way round this for automation: record short recruiter clips in batches and combine them with automated text overlays.

### Gaps
- No engagement data for individual German career accounts (StepStone, Indeed DE, XING, karriere.at, karrierebibel) on Instagram Reels. Their Instagram profiles could not be analysed without scraping.
- No comparison of performance between career content types (job of the day vs tips vs salary).
- No reliable figures for Instagram follower counts of karriereguru or Mr. Bewerbung. The ranking only gives TikTok numbers.

## 2. Hook patterns (first 1–3 s), Reel length, loop, text vs subtitles, pacing

### Takeaway
The largest current study (Socialinsider, 9.77M brand Reels, H1 2026) finds 45–60 s performs best on engagement (0.35%) and median views (10,374). Reels from 30 s up to 3 min hold steady, below 30 s performs clearly worse, and above 3 min performance roughly halves. Very short 7–15 s Reels are recommended for completion and loops, but only weaker sources back this. On hooks there are hardly any reliable Instagram studies. The most robust data (Emplifi, on Facebook Reels) shows that human speech or a face in the first 3 seconds improves retention more than text alone.

### Cited Findings
- **[STUDIE] Socialinsider (published 14.07.2026, updated 20.10.2026), 9,771,822 organic Reels from brands with 1k–1M followers, Jan–Jun 2026:**

  | Length | Engagement rate | Median views |
  |---|---|---|
  | 0–30 s | 0.28% | 4,700 |
  | 30–45 s | 0.30% | 8,564 |
  | **45–60 s** | **0.35%** | **10,374** |
  | 60–90 s | 0.30% | 9,790 |
  | 90–120 s | 0.30% | 8,000 |
  | 120–180 s | 0.33% | 9,000 |
  | >180 s | 0.15% | 4,428 |

  - Methodology: engagement rate is (likes + comments) / followers. Saves and sends are not included. The data is correlational and has small inconsistencies (sample sums and tier definitions don't match exactly).
  - The study recommends "45–60 s as the default for content meant to reach new audiences" and keeping Reels under 3 minutes.
  - Source: [Socialinsider Reels length](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-length/)
- **Limits:**
  - **[OFFIZIELL]** Reels can be up to 20 minutes long (Socialinsider citing the Help Center). Reels **over 3 minutes** are not shown in the Reels tab and not recommended to non-followers. — [Socialinsider](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-length/)
  - Older (June 2024): Instagram said at the time that Reels **over 90 seconds** would not be recommended to non-followers. The current limit is 3 minutes. — [Social Media Today, 05.06.2024](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-advice-on-single-word-ctas-and-longer-reels/718151/)
- **[MEINUNG/small study] Short Reels:**
  - Socialbu recommends 7–15 s for discovery and reach. A cited analysis of 500 viral Reels found the highest completion rates at 7–15 s. That analysis has no primary source.
  - SEOWorks (n = 68) found a moderate negative correlation between length and views (-0.38) and calls 2–3 minutes the weakest zone. This contradicts Socialinsider in part. The sample is very small.
  - Sources: [Socialbu](https://socialbu.com/blog/instagram-reels-length); [SEOWorks](https://www.seoworks.co.uk/downloads/whats-the-worst-length-for-an-instagram-reel/)
- **[STUDIE] Metricool 2026:** Average watch time on Reels rose from 4 to 8.5 seconds year on year. — [OnlineMarketing.de](https://onlinemarketing.de/social-media-marketing/instagram-studie-2026-formate-beste-performance)
- **[STUDIE, Facebook not Instagram] Emplifi (10,110 Facebook Reels from 704 brand pages):** Human speech in the first 3 seconds improves retention. A person on screen in that window raises 10-second retention by about 10%. Text overlays help too, but less than voice or a face. I took this from the search summary and did not check the original study. — reported in search results via [opus.pro](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-instagram-reels-length) and [nestscale](https://nestscale.com/blog/increase-tiktok-hook-rate.html). The original Emplifi study is not linked; verify before citing.
- **[MEINUNG] Hook practice from creator guides:**
  - Put the hook in the first 1–1.5 s and show text on screen almost immediately.
  - Keep first-frame text short. Recommendations range from 3–5 words to under 12 words.
  - Use frequent visual changes such as cuts or overlays.
  - To test, change only the first 2–3 seconds.
  - "Skip rate" (viewers who drop off before 3 s) was reportedly added to Insights in 2025. I could not confirm this officially.
  - Sources: [nestscale](https://nestscale.com/blog/increase-tiktok-hook-rate.html); [creatorstack](https://creatorstack-io.pages.dev/blog/instagram-reels-retention-heatmaps-skip-rate-2026); [dev.to Hook Engineering](https://dev.to/bulkdl/hook-engineering-the-science-of-the-first-3-seconds-in-short-form-video-2026-2jp9)

### Inferences
- **Two lengths for automated daily Reels:**
  - (a) 7–15 s loop reels for job of the day and "Wusstest du?" facts: high completion and replay, so watch time per reach stays high.
  - (b) 30–60 s tips reels (3 Fehler, interview questions, salary negotiation) for saves and sends, matching the 45–60 s finding.
  - Avoid going over 3 minutes.
- **Hook templates for career content (German, derived suggestions, not tested):**
  - Number or salary in the first frame: "68.000 € als SAP-Berater in Freiburg?"
  - Error frame: "3 Fehler, die deinen Lebenslauf sofort aussortieren"
  - Question: "Was antwortest du auf ‚Warum wollen Sie wechseln?'"
  - Myth: "Mythos: Wer kündigt, muss sofort was Neues haben"
  - Regional: "Tuttlingen hat mehr Medizintechnik-Firmen als …". Check any fact like this before use.
- Burned-in on-screen text is useful because many people watch without sound. Speech or a face early on still appears to carry more weight. A short spoken intro from a recruiter, filmed once and reusable, would combine both.
- Scene changes every 1–2 s are common creator practice. I found no robust study on this.

### Gaps
- No Instagram-specific primary study on hook types (question vs number vs "Fehler").
- No reliable data comparing subtitles with free-form on-screen text.
- The original Emplifi study is not verified, and its "+10% retention" figure is a secondary report.
- No official confirmation of a "skip rate" metric.

## 3. Instagram's own guidance: originality, text-only or slideshow Reels, templates, watermarks, Trial Reels

### Takeaway
**[OFFIZIELL]** Since **30.04.2026**, the originality rules cover photos and carousels as well as Reels. Accounts that "primarily" post unoriginal content are no longer recommended to non-followers. This is assessed account-wide on a rolling 30-day window, and status can be checked and appealed in Account Status. Content you design yourself counts as original, including a "how-to guide" and text that adds context. Re-uploads with only a border, watermark, subtitles or a credit do not. I found **no official statement** that text-heavy or slideshow Reels are generally downranked. Trial Reels have existed since December 2024 and are shown to non-followers first.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFIZIELL] Instagram Creators blog "Rewarding original creators" (30.04.2026):**
  - "accounts that primarily post unoriginal content in photos or carousel posts, in addition to reels, will no longer be shown in places where we recommend content."
  - "This update does not impact how we show people content from accounts they follow."
  - "Original content is work that you wholly created or reflects your unique perspective." Examples include content you designed, such as a photo series or a **how-to guide**.
  - Material edits count, e.g. "unique text on the content itself that provides more context than just describing what's happening" and "creative graphics that offer new information."
  - Unoriginal: "Re-uploading someone else's work … without adding meaningful creative input". Adding "a border, watermark, subtitles, or a credit in the captions" does not make content original.
  - Recommendation eligibility returns when "a significant percentage" of recent posts in a rolling 30-day window are original. The post does not define the threshold.
  - Status can be checked and appealed in Account Status.
  - Instagram says 75% of recommendations in the US now come from original posts.
  - Source: [creators.instagram.com](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram)
- **[MEINUNG/secondary]** The rule was first introduced for Reels in 2024 and extended account-wide in 2026. — [Digital Camera World](https://www.digitalcameraworld.com/tech/social-media/two-years-later-instagram-is-finally-giving-photographers-the-same-protection-as-videographers-with-this-key-change); [Engadget (2024)](https://www.engadget.com/instagrams-algorithm-overhaul-will-reward-original-content-and-penalize-aggregators-130018977.html)
- **[MEINUNG]** Mosseri's 2026 priorities reportedly include "Continue to improve ranking for originality" (a Threads memo, reported by a secondary source). — [creatorflow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-algorithm/)
- **Text-only and slideshow Reels:** I found no official source saying text-only or slideshow Reels are downranked for low quality. The search turned up only blog speculation, some of it in the opposite direction. — [Search result overview, e.g. Planoly](https://planoly.com/blog/new-instagram-updates-this-week)
- **[OFFIZIELL] Recommendation guidelines:** Engagement bait and clickbait are among the content types Instagram avoids recommending. Repeat violators can lose recommendation eligibility for a period of time. — [Instagram Help: Recommendations Guidelines](https://help.instagram.com/313829416281232); [creators.instagram.com Recommendations & Originality](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-Originality)
- **[OFFIZIELL] Trial Reels (10.12.2024):** Shown to non-followers first. The creator can choose to share automatically with followers if the trial performs well. Rolled out to professional accounts. — [creators.instagram.com Trial Reels](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels); [Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/10/instagram-rolls-out-trial-reels-that-arent-shown-to-a-creators-followers)
- **[MEINUNG, unverified]** Some 2025–26 guides say Trial Reels require a public professional account with at least 1,000 followers. One cites a Mosseri statement from July 2025. — [postfa.st](https://postfa.st/blog/instagram-trial-reels)
- **[MEINUNG]** German 2026 Reels guides advise against watermarks, for example TikTok logos on cross-posts. — [skill-sprinters](https://skill-sprinters.de/blog/social-media/instagram-reels-strategie/)

### Inferences
- **Template-generated reels are not unoriginal in themselves**, provided the content is the agency's own: its own job data, its own tips, its own regional facts, its own graphics. Under the official definition, a designed "how-to guide" and "creative graphics that offer new information" count as original. The risks are reused stock clips, third-party content and duplicate reels with identical structure. The official text does not cover these explicitly.
- The **"low-quality template"** risk is not about originality but about watch time. Identical, monotonous slideshows probably lose retention. Recommendations: vary the visuals (colours, B-roll from the region, a recruiter face), never reuse watermarked TikTok exports, and use licensed or Instagram audio.
- Check Account Status regularly, because the 30-day rolling window applies account-wide.
- Trial Reels are a good fit for A/B testing hook variants with an automated pipeline, if the account has access. Check the follower threshold in the account itself.

### Gaps
- No official statement on whether automatically generated (AI or template) videos are classed differently.
- No official threshold for "primarily" or "significant percentage".
- Current Trial Reels eligibility (follower threshold) is not officially confirmed.

## 4. Series formats, posting rhythm, cannibalisation (daily Reel plus daily carousel), best times

### Takeaway
Weekly theme days are common practice, but I found no study showing they perform better. On frequency, Instagram's official guidance reported by t3n is 2–3 Reels per week. Daily is possible, but quality and originality matter more. There is no data on cannibalisation between a daily Reel and a daily carousel. Reels and carousels are rated differently (Reels for reach, carousels for saves), and Reels collect views more slowly. For Germany there are no specific best-time data. Global studies point to weekday evenings around 19–21 h (Metricool) or weekday mornings 8–12 h (SocialPilot, Reels).

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFIZIELL, via t3n]** Instagram recommends two to three Reels per week. This comes from a search summary; I did not fetch the original article. — [t3n](https://t3n.de/news/?p=1635697)
- **[STUDIE] Metricool 2026:**
  - Brands are posting more of every format: Reels +36%, carousels +24%, single images +12%.
  - Images and carousels collect 76% of their views within 72 hours. Reels collect only 65%, so they keep collecting views for longer.
  - Source: [OnlineMarketing.de](https://onlinemarketing.de/social-media-marketing/instagram-studie-2026-formate-beste-performance)
- **[STUDIE] Best times:**
  - Metricool 2026 (24M posts, global): best results between 19 and 21 h. — [Metricool DE](https://metricool.com/de/beste-zeit-zum-posten-auf-instagram/)
  - SocialPilot (Reels 2026): Monday to Thursday, 8–12 h. — [SocialPilot DE](https://www.socialpilot.co/de/insights/best-time-to-post-reels-on-instagram)
  - Buffer 2026 (9.6M posts): weekday evenings. — [Buffer DE](https://buffer.com/de/resources/beste-zeit-instagram-posten)
  - Adobe Express (June 2026, 20,247 Reels, US time zone): Reels posted at 3 a.m. ET got 2.1x the views of 9 p.m. posts. This does not transfer to DE. — [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/instagram-reels-posted-at-3-a-m-gain-2-1-times-the-views-of-9-p-m-posts/)
- **[MEINUNG]** Global studies can be hours off for a German audience. The common advice is to use your own Insights. — [blabla.ai](https://blabla.ai/de/blog/best-time-to-post-on-instagram)
- **[OFFIZIELL]** Likes matter more for content shown to followers, sends more for content shown to non-followers. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)
- **[MEINUNG/Media]** ingenieur.de warns against "Job-Spam" and irregular activity in recruiting accounts. — [ingenieur.de](https://www.ingenieur.de/karriere/arbeitsleben/fachkraefte-finden-auf-instagram-so-kann-es-gehen/)

### Inferences
- **Cannibalisation:** If the daily carousel is the job ad and the Reel is the *same* job, followers see the same thing twice, likes per reach drops and "Job-Spam" sets in. Better to separate the roles:
  - **Carousel** for the concrete job post, which works with followers, gets saves and drives applications.
  - **Reel** mainly for tips, salary and regional content that reaches non-followers through sends. Bring in a top job only 1–2 times a week as a reel.
- **Spacing suggestion (untested):** carousel in the morning (about 7–8 h, commuting time for skilled workers) and Reel in the evening (about 18–20 h), or the other way round. Then compare Insights over 4–6 weeks.
- **Suggested weekly theme days (derived, not evidence-based):**
  - Mo: Bewerbungstipp (Lebenslauf/Anschreiben)
  - Di: Top-Job der Woche (Reel version)
  - Mi: Gehalt (ranges for specific roles in the region)
  - Do: "3 Fehler" or myth-busting (Vorstellungsgespräch)
  - Fr: "Wusstest du?" (Arbeitsrecht/Kündigungsfrist/Probezeit) or a regional job-market fact
  - Sa/So: recruiter behind the scenes, or a light FAQ ("Kostet mich ein Personalvermittler was?")
- Daily Reels go beyond Instagram's 2–3 per week. Automation only makes sense if every reel is distinct, original content.

### Gaps
- No study on daily Reels plus daily carousel, or on cannibalisation between formats.
- No Germany-specific best-time study, and none for the target groups (skilled workers in shifts, IT, banking).
- No evidence that theme days or series lift reach. The only suggested benefits (follower expectation, production efficiency) are plausible but unmeasured.

## 5. CTAs for sends and saves, and what counts as engagement bait

### Takeaway
**[OFFIZIELL]** Instagram won't recommend content that "explicitly asks for engagement through shares, comments, tags, or other actions" with the aim of "hacking" reach. Its example is "comment with a specific word, number, or emoji" (June 2024). Instagram then clarified that open-ended questions and CTAs that encourage real discussion are fine, and tools like ManyChat remain allowed. The test is intent. Natural, content-related prompts like "Speichern für dein nächstes Gespräch" or "Schick das jemandem, der gerade wechselt" are probably fine. Mechanical prompts like "Tagge 3 Freunde" or "Kommentiere JOB" are risky.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFIZIELL]**
  - Instagram warned against "hack[ing] engagement" through shares, comments, tags or other actions. Its example was "asking people to comment with a specific word, number, or emoji". Open-ended questions and calls to action stay OK, since Instagram wants content that "inspires people to engage in real discussion." — [Social Media Today, 02.06.2024](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-says-using-certain-ctas-can-impact-post-reach/717732/)
  - In the clarification, Instagram said there were no changes to the recommendation guidelines and that ManyChat-style tools remain usable. "Earnestly engaging with your audience in the comments and DMs" and genuine lead generation are not engagement bait. The problem is content that "attempts to game the system by eliciting specific interactions." — [Social Media Today, 05.06.2024](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-advice-on-single-word-ctas-and-longer-reels/718151/)
  - Engagement bait is part of the recommendation guidelines, and repeat violations can make an account ineligible for recommendation for a period. — [Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/313829416281232); [creators.instagram.com](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-Originality)
- **[STUDIE]** Posts with a question get 36.7% more comments (Metricool 2026). — [OnlineMarketing.de](https://onlinemarketing.de/social-media-marketing/instagram-studie-2026-formate-beste-performance)
- **[OFFIZIELL]** Sends per reach is one of the three most important signals, and especially relevant for reach among non-followers. Instagram Insights does not show sends per reach directly; guides recommend calculating shares ÷ reach. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/); [Lindsey Gamble](https://www.lindseygamble.com/blog/unpacking-instagrams-focus-on-sends-per-reach-a-timeline-why-it-matters-and-tips-for-creators-brands)
- **[MEINUNG]** Commentators disagree on "tag a friend" and "share this". Some see a simple "share with a friend" as acceptable; others see "tag a friend" as manipulative. — [Search result overview incl. nowbam](https://nowbam.com/three-things-that-can-hurt-your-instagram-engagement-and-what-to-do-instead/)
- **Older background:** Facebook's 2017 framework already demoted vote, react, share, tag and comment bait. — [Search Engine Journal](https://searchenginejournal.com/facebook-demoting-engagement-bait/228071)

### Inferences
- **Probably safe CTAs**, because they are tied to the content and to a real use:
  - "Speichern – fürs nächste Vorstellungsgespräch."
  - "Schick das an jemanden, der gerade über einen Jobwechsel nachdenkt."
  - "Welche Frage hat dich im Interview am meisten überrascht?" (open question)
  - "Mehr Jobs in der Region: Link in Bio" or "Schreib uns eine DM, wenn du in IT/ERP wechseln willst" (genuine lead generation)
- **Risky:**
  - "Tagge 3 Freunde"
  - "Kommentiere JOB für den Link", unless it is genuinely needed and used sparingly
  - "Like, wenn …"
  - "Teile, damit mehr Leute …" with no link to the content
- **Building send triggers into the content:** Salary comparisons, regional facts ("Wusstest du, dass in VS-Villingen …?") and "Fehler" lists have built-in send potential, because they are relevant to others. Content-driven sends are better than relying on the CTA.

### Gaps
- No official list of allowed and forbidden CTA wordings. Instagram deliberately does not publish one.
- No data on the effect of save or send CTAs on reach, for DACH or anywhere else.
