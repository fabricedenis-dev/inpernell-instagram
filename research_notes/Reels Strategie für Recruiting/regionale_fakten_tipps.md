# Regionale Fakten & Karriere-Tipps für Recruiting-Reels (SBK, Tuttlingen, Rottweil, Freiburg)

Research date: 18.11.2026. Verification levels:
- **[V]** = I read it myself in the primary source (PDF or press release fetched directly)
- **[S]** = taken from a secondary source or search snippet; check the original before publishing
- **[L]** = statutory text (stable, primary source)

## 1. Regionaler Arbeitsmarkt (Arbeitslosigkeit, Stellen, Engpässe, Cluster)

### Takeaway
The region's labour market is moderately weak but improving. In October 2026 the unemployment rate was 3.3 % in Kreis Rottweil, 5.0 % in Schwarzwald-Baar-Kreis, 5.5 % in Kreis Tuttlingen, 4.2 % in the Freiburg district and 6.3 % nationally. The IHK SBH business climate index reached its highest level in three years (109 points, November 2026). The most striking Reel material: in the Freiburg district there are 12 unemployed people per vacancy in office/management jobs but only 1.5 in production-technology jobs.

### Cited Findings
**Agentur für Arbeit Rottweil–Villingen-Schwenningen (October 2026, published 30.10.2026)** [V]
- District: 13,475 unemployed, rate 4.7 % (−0.1 pp vs. September). That is 437 fewer than in September but 293 more than in October 2025. — [NRWZ, 30.10.2026](https://www.nrwz.de/wirtschaft/arbeitsmarkt-im-kreis-rottweil-arbeitslosenquote-sinkt-im-oktober-auf-33-prozent-585449.html)
- Kreis Rottweil 2,691 unemployed / **3.3 %**. Schwarzwald-Baar-Kreis 6,153 / **5.0 %**. Kreis Tuttlingen 4,631 / **5.5 %**. All three are October 2026. — [NRWZ](https://www.nrwz.de/wirtschaft/arbeitsmarkt-im-kreis-rottweil-arbeitslosenquote-sinkt-im-oktober-auf-33-prozent-585449.html)
- 3,581 vacancies registered in the district (−85). Employers reported 800 new vacancies in October. Quote from agency head Sylvia Scholz: "die Suche nach einem neuen Arbeitsplatz bleibt herausfordernd". — [NRWZ](https://www.nrwz.de/wirtschaft/arbeitsmarkt-im-kreis-rottweil-arbeitslosenquote-sinkt-im-oktober-auf-33-prozent-585449.html)
- Reel idea: "Rottweil has the lowest unemployment rate in the region (3.3 %), Tuttlingen the highest (5.5 %)." Surprising, given that Tuttlingen is the MedTech capital.

**Agentur für Arbeit Freiburg (Freiburg city, Breisgau-Hochschwarzwald, Emmendingen), October 2026** [V]
- 16,649 unemployed, rate **4.2 %** (Sept. 4.3 %, Oct. 2025 4.3 %). Long-term unemployed: 4,174 (−8.0 % year on year). — [BA-Statistik Arbeitsmarktreport Freiburg Okt. 2026 (PDF)](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/vor-ort/datei/arbeitsmarktberichtoktober26_ba287535.pdf)
- Registered vacancies: 4,309 in stock (−7.0 % year on year). New vacancies in October: 823 (−21 % year on year). — same source
- **Unemployed per registered vacancy, by occupational segment (October 2026):** cleaning 12.8; company management/organisation (office) **12.0**; IT and natural-science services **7.9**; total **3.9**; health 2.5; manufacturing 2.4; construction 1.5; **production-technology occupations 1.5**. — same source (p. 13)
- Unemployed IT and natural-science specialists: +18.0 % year on year (964 people). — same source
- Next release: 27.11.2026. — same source

**Germany and Baden-Württemberg** [S]
- Germany October 2026: 6.3 %. Baden-Württemberg October 2026: 4.6 %. — [BA Presse Okt. 2026](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/presse/2026-41-arbeitsmarkt-im-oktober-2026) (figures from search snippet; check)

**Engpassberufe (BA Fachkräfteengpassanalyse 2025, published 02.09.2026)** [V]
- 157 occupations with shortages in 2025 (2024: 163). The strongest shortages are in nursing and health care, followed by construction and skilled trades. Cooks, professional drivers and educators are also sought after. — [BA Presseinfo 32/2026](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/presse/2026-32-fachkraefte-bleiben-knapp-zuwanderung-gewinnt-weiter-an-bedeutung)
- **No longer shortage occupations nationally:** cutting metalworking (spanende Metallbearbeitung, i.e. Zerspanung), IT sales, software development. — same source. Myth-buster Reel: "Software developers are always wanted? Not anymore, according to the BA."
- Half of all registered skilled-worker vacancies (annual average of about 402,000) are in shortage occupations. The share of foreign employees in shortage occupations rose from about 7 % (2014) to about 14 %. — same source

**IHK Schwarzwald-Baar-Heuberg, Autumn 2026 economic report (11.11.2026)** [V]
- Business climate index at **109 points**, the highest in three years. It is above the Baden-Württemberg average for the first time since early summer 2023. — [IHK SBH Medieninfo 11.11.2026](https://www.ihk.de/sbh/presse/aktuelle-pressemeldungen/medieninformationen22026/neuer-inhalt11-11-ihk-wirtschaftslagebericht-herbst-2026-7224226)
- 34 % rate their situation as good, 18 % as bad. 20 % expect better business, 17 % worse. Almost one in four expects more exports. The IHK expects a stable labour market but "no broad hiring wave". Fachkräftemangel is becoming more important as a risk again (no percentage given). — same source

**Tuttlingen / MedTech cluster (IHK SBH Steckbrief Gesundheitswirtschaft, as of July 2026)** [V]
- Tuttlingen (38,275 inhabitants) is described within the industry as the "Weltzentrum der Medizintechnik". More than 200 companies directly manufacture surgical and medical-technology products, from scalpels to endoscopes and laser systems. — [IHK SBH Steckbrief 2025/26 (PDF)](https://www.ihk.de/blueprint/servlet/resource/blob/7130270/65ff7aa6d49deacdbe971fb692f219a3/steckbrief-gesundheitswirtschaft-2025-data.pdf)
- 14,178 employees subject to social insurance work in "Medizinische Produkte und Geräte" in the SBH region (39.2 % of the health economy; data as of March 2026). The health economy as a whole has 36,060 employees, **16.6 % of all employees** in the region. — same source
- 215 IHK member companies are in the "Medizinische Produkte und Geräte" segment. — same source
- Bonus fact: Bad Dürrheim is the only place in BW that holds the "Heilklimatischer Kurort", "Sole-Heilbad" and "Kneipp-Kurort" designations at the same time. — same source
- Reel idea: "Roughly 1 in 3 people in Tuttlingen works in MedTech." Do **not** use this: it is not verified. Use the 14,178 figure instead.

### Inferences
- Content angle: the job market is split. Office and management jobs face heavy competition (12 applicants per vacancy in Freiburg), while production-technology and construction jobs have very little (1.5). The message for skilled trades and production workers is "you are in a strong negotiating position".
- Tuttlingen's high unemployment rate alongside the MedTech cluster suggests structural change (automotive suppliers, metal). This is an interpretation, not a source statement.

### Gaps
- No Kreis-level vacancy counts for SBK, Tuttlingen or Rottweil (only the district total of 3,581).
- Baden-Württemberg-specific shortage occupations: the BA interactive application was not checked ([Engpassanalyse](https://statistik.arbeitsagentur.de/DE/Navigation/Statistiken/Interaktive-Statistiken/Fachkraeftebedarf/Engpassanalyse-Nav.html)).
- IHK Südlicher Oberrhein (Freiburg) economic report autumn 2026: not researched.
- Unverified numbers about Tuttlingen circulating online ("400–500 MedTech companies", "Karl Storz approx. 3,000 employees in Tuttlingen") are undated or secondary. Do not use them.
- Automotive suppliers in the region: no current hard facts collected.

## 2. Gehälter (Entgeltatlas, StepStone, Region vs. Deutschland)

### Takeaway
Baden-Württemberg has the second-highest median pay of any state for full-time employees: €4,546 gross per month in 2025, compared with €4,217 nationally (+5.1 % on 2024). Occupation-specific Entgeltatlas figures for Zerspanungsmechaniker and similar roles could not be retrieved automatically and must be looked up manually.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 median for full-time employees in Germany: **€4,217 gross per month**, +€203 (+5.1 %) on 2024, mainly due to collective wage agreements. Published 20.07.2026. [V] — [BA Presseinfo 27/2026](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/presse/2026-27-entgelte-der-vollzeitbeschaeftigten-sind-im-jahr-2025-deutlich-gestiegen)
- Top states: Hamburg €4,768, **Baden-Württemberg €4,546**, Hessen €4,530. Lowest: Mecklenburg-Vorpommern €3,497. [V] — same source
- Average pay by qualification: without vocational qualification €3,133; with vocational qualification €4,069; with university degree €6,146. Gender gap €309 (men €4,328, women €4,019). [V] — same source
- StepStone Gehaltsreport 2026 (2025 data): German gross median €53,900 per year. [S] — [StepStone Gehaltsreport](https://www.stepstone.de/gehaltsreport/)
- StepStone: Baden-Württemberg median **€58,500 per year**, second behind Hamburg (≈ €60,000), ahead of Hessen and Bayern. [S] — [ING Wissen](https://www.ing.de/wissen/gehaltsstudie-stepstone-2026/); [Statista](https://de.statista.com/infografik/amp/35671/bruttojahresgehaelter-von-vollzeitbeschaeftigten-in-deutschland)
- StepStone (as cited by TextilWirtschaft): on average a pay rise brings about +4 %, a job change about +8%. Men gain about 10 % from switching, women about 5 %. **Edition/year unclear.** [S] — [TextilWirtschaft](https://www.textilwirtschaft.de/fashion-management/jobwechsel-bringt-8-mehr-gehalt-doch-maenner-profitieren-staerker-als-frauen-77644)

### Inferences
- Reel hook: "Your salary in BW is about €330 per month above the national median. Do you know your occupation's figure?" Then point to the free Entgeltatlas.
- Always say "median", not "average". Both BA and StepStone report medians.

### Gaps
- **Entgeltatlas occupation figures** (Zerspanungsmechaniker, Industriemechaniker, Konstrukteur, Bankkaufmann, SAP/ERP consultant, BW vs. Germany) were not retrievable because the tool requires JavaScript. Look them up manually at https://web.arbeitsagentur.de/entgeltatlas/ (filters: occupation, BW, Vollzeit, data year 2025). Secondary figures found online (e.g. Zerspanungsmechaniker "approx. €4,000") are unreliable. Do not use them.
- No regional salary data at Kreis level (SBK, TUT, RW).

## 3. Gehaltsverhandlung & Bewerbung (tips with evidence, legal basics, myths)

### Takeaway
Hard data is thinner than career guides suggest. Usable facts: half of Germans plan to negotiate, a job change brings roughly twice as much as an internal raise, and recruiters spend about 43 seconds on a CV. The legal basics (Probezeit, Kündigungsfristen, Arbeitszeugnis) are solid myth-buster material.

### Cited Findings
**Negotiation**
- Kununu survey (November 2024, n=1,083): 54 % planned to negotiate their salary in 2025. **Data is from 2024; flag accordingly.** [S] — [ingenieur.de](https://www.ingenieur.de/karriere/gehalt/gehalt-ist-verhandelbar-und-immer-mehr-machen-es-auch/)
- PageGroup Talent Trends 2025 (n≈19,000): 45 % of German respondents raised the topic of a pay rise, and 24 % got one (European figure: 18 %). [S] — [Tagesspiegel / Studienbericht](https://www.tagesspiegel.de/wirtschaft/die-deutschen-verhandeln-besonders-erfolgreich-ihr-gehalt-13638874.html)
- StepStone: the most common reasons people do not negotiate are uncertainty about their arguments (43 %) and fear of seeming ungrateful (42 %). Almost three quarters of under-30s talk openly about pay, versus 48 % of over-30s. [S] — [humanresourcesmanager.de](https://www.humanresourcesmanager.de/recruiting/offen-ueber-das-gehalt-sprechen-fuer-die-u30-generation-kein-tabu-mehr/)
- XING/forsa 2026: 34 % of employees are open to a job change. [S] — [onlinemarketing.de](https://onlinemarketing.de/karriere/human-resources/34-prozent-sind-offen-fuer-jobwechsel)
- Anchoring tip: no regional or German primary study was collected. If used, present it as general negotiation psychology only, without a number.

**Application**
- StepStone eye-tracking study: recruiters look at a CV for an average of **43 seconds**, about 22 seconds of it on work experience. **Date unclear (StepStone Austria, older).** [S] — [iqb.de](https://iqb.de/karrieremagazin/bewerbung/darauf-achten-personaler-wirklich-der-lebenslauf/)
- Softgarden: 53 % of applicants said they would have applied if no cover letter had been required (date unclear). A 2020 study found the cover letter optional at two thirds of companies (**old**). [S] — [Softgarden Blog](https://web2026.softgarden.com/de/tag/candidate-experience/)
- Softgarden 2025 (n=6,929, May–July 2025): the share of applicants using AI has more than tripled since 2023. ChatGPT is by far the most used tool (85.8 %). [S] — [Lebensmittel Zeitung](https://www.lebensmittelzeitung.net/handel/karriere/recruiting-ki-einsatz-im-bewerbungsprozess-186354)

**Legal basics / myths** [L]
- Probezeit can be agreed for at most 6 months, with a notice period of **2 weeks** during it (§ 622 Abs. 3 BGB). Outside the Probezeit, the basic notice period is 4 weeks to the 15th or to the end of a month (§ 622 Abs. 1). For the employer it lengthens with length of service (§ 622 Abs. 2). — [§ 622 BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__622.html)
- BAG 6 AZR 519/07: a 6-month Probezeit is allowed even for simple jobs. — [Hensche](https://www.hensche.de/Rechtsanwalt_Arbeitsrecht_Urteile_Kuendigung_Probezeit_BAG_6AZR519-07.html)
- BAG 2 AZR 275/23 (05.12.2024): if a fixed-term contract has a Probezeit covering the whole term, the 2-week notice period does not apply; the basic 4-week period does. — [beck-aktuell](https://www.beck-aktuell.de/heute-im-recht/rechtsprechung/bag-2azr27523-befristet-arbeitsverhaeltnis-probezeit-dauer-2025-02-24)
- Employees have a right to a written Arbeitszeugnis. On request it must be a qualified reference, and it must be clear and must not contain coded wording ("Geheimcode verboten", § 109 Abs. 2 GewO). — [§ 109 GewO](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gewo/__109.html)
- Probezeit and the Kündigungsschutzgesetz are separate things. The KSchG only applies after 6 months of employment and in businesses with more than 10 employees (§ 1, § 23 KSchG). — [§ 1 KSchG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__1.html)
- AGG: a job advert must not discriminate by race/ethnic origin, sex, religion, disability, age or sexual identity (§§ 1, 11 AGG). — [AGG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/agg/)

### Inferences
- Strongest Reel: "Changing jobs brings about 8 %, a pay rise about 4 % (StepStone)". It needs the edition checked first.
- "Coded wording in Arbeitszeugnisse is prohibited by law" is a good myth-buster, since in practice certain set phrases are still in use.

### Gaps
- No German primary study on "when to name a number first" or on anchoring.
- Overtime: the BAG ruling (e.g. 5 AZR 359/21, 2022, burden of proof for overtime) was not checked in this session.
- No current (2025/26) German eye-tracking study on CVs, and nothing on CV length (1 vs. 2 pages).

## 4. Direktvermittlung vs. Zeitarbeit (facts for job seekers)

### Takeaway
The legal rules for temporary agency work (Zeitarbeit) are clearly defined: equal pay at the latest after 9 months, a maximum of 18 months at the same client company, and a statutory wage floor. With Direktvermittlung, the candidate becomes an employee of the client company from day one. Market numbers are only available from secondary sources.

### Cited Findings
- Maximum assignment length: generally **18 consecutive months** with the same client company. Collective agreements in the client's industry can set a different limit. After a break of at least 3 months, the count starts again. — [§ 1 Abs. 1b AÜG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/a_g/__1.html) [L]
- Equal pay: temp workers are entitled to the same pay as the client's permanent staff. Collective agreements may deviate from this for at most the **first 9 months** of an assignment, and longer only with industry-specific supplementary agreements (Branchenzuschläge). — [§ 8 AÜG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/a_g/__8.html) [L]
- Wage floor in temporary agency work: €14.53 per hour since 01.03.2025, according to an industry source. [S] Check the current 2026 value. — [personaldienstleister.de](https://personaldienstleister.de/?p=71420)
- About 622,000 temporary agency workers in June 2025 (≈2 % of employment), according to a blog citing BA statistics. [S] Not verified against the original. — [ulmato.de](https://www.ulmato.de/jobsuche/zeitarbeit/)
- An evaluation found that the share of assignments lasting more than 18 months fell from 18 % to 11 % after the reform. Report not clearly identified. [S] — [HTWG Konstanz PDF](https://www.htwg-konstanz.de/fileadmin/pub/ou/kicg/News/AuA0924-AR-Haag_Putze.pdf)

### Inferences
- Neutral Reel framing: "Zeitarbeit: your employer is the agency. Direktvermittlung: your employer is the company. Both are legal, but the contract partner is different." Probezeit, notice periods and the pay basis all depend on who the contract partner is.
- Direktvermittlung is usually free for the candidate. **No source collected**, so state it only as a statement about the agency's own model.

### Gaps
- No primary source for the takeover rate (Klebeeffekt) from temporary agency work into permanent employment.
- No current official BA count of temporary agency workers in BW or the region.
